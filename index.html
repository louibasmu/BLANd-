About the game

A play on the card shedding game “Old Maid” mixed with the classic chaos game Uno. The player can use special cards to give themselves an advantage while trying to obtain the most points(pairs) at the end of N rounds.

How to play

Goal

Score the most points after all rounds. Every pair you make is worth 1 point. Anyone still holding a Joker when the round ends loses 4 points per Joker.

The deck

40 pair cards (A–10, four suits), the special cards (12 by default; change them in Special Cards setup), plus 1–3 Jokers depending on the round. All cards are dealt out to the 4 players. Pairs in your starting hand are discarded (and scored) right away.

Before each round

Your hand is dealt in random order. Arrange it however you like, then lock it. Once the round starts you can't reorder, and any card you take or receive goes to the end of your hand.

Your turn

On your turn you either draw a face-down card from ANY other player who still has cards (pick the player, then the card), or play a special card from your hand.

  • Make a pair → go again. Draw again or play a special.
  • Draw a special card → you may play it right away, or keep it for later (keeping it ends your turn).
  • Play a special and don't make a pair → your turn is over — except Skip and Reverse: after playing one of those you still take a card.
  • Draw anything else → your turn is over.

Special cards

Border colour shows rarity: light blue = Common (3+ copies), green = Uncommon (2), purple = Rare (1)

  • Skip (3) – the next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse (3) – turn order flips direction.
  • Give (2) – give one card from your hand to any player. Yes, you can give away a Joker.
  • Freeze (2) – pick a player; they miss their next 2 turns.
  • Angel (1) – pick a player; you learn whether they hold a Joker (yes/no).
  • Vision (1) – pick a player and a card position (the numbers under their cards). You see that card, then take it or leave it. If taking it makes a pair you go again; otherwise your turn ends.

End of round

A player who runs out of cards is out for the round and safe from the Joker. They can't be drawn from, given cards, or targeted, and they don't rejoin until the next round.

The round ends when every pair card has been matched. Jokers left in hands cost 4 points each. Players never leave the game – everyone plays every round.
